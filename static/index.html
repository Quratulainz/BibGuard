Questions

Why was my real reference marked "Not found"?

Books, theses and some conference papers are missing from Crossref and OpenAlex. "Not found" means check manually, not fake.

Is this the same as Scopus or Web of Science?

No. BibGuard uses publisher-registered metadata from Crossref and OpenAlex. A Scopus badge appears only when the site owner has connected a Scopus key. Web of Science is not included.

What happens to my files?

BibGuard reads files to extract references and sends lookup terms or identifiers to scholarly metadata services. Extracted references and check results are temporarily retained so your browser can retrieve them after a background check. BibGuard also stores usage records, account information, and any feedback you submit.
